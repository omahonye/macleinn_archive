# MacLéinn Archive

**Documenting the history of student representation across Ireland.**

MacLéinn Archive is an independent, student-run project collecting and preserving the histories of Students’ Unions across Irish universities and colleges.

From Presidents and sabbatical officers to long-forgotten positions and former unions, the archive aims to trace who represented students, when they served, and how their unions changed over time.

The archive is a work in progress. Gaps remain, particularly in older records, and the collection will continue to grow as new sources are found.

## Explore the archive

Use the **sidebar** to browse by Students’ Union, or use the **search bar** to look for a person by name and discover where and when they served.

*MacLéinn Archive is an independent project and is not affiliated with any university, college or students’ union.*
