# CML OnG Officer History

A historical listing of Comhaltas na Mac Léinn, Ollscoil na Gaillimhe (University of Galway SU) officers from 1964 to present.

## About

This record compiles the known executive officers of the Comhaltas na Mac Léinn, Ollscoil na Gaillimhe from 1960 to present.

**Sources:** compiled from archived versions of su.universityofgalway.ie, personal records, and alumni input.
